CLINICAL TRIAL RECORD
Registry identifier: NCT03947138

Title: A Study of GC3107(BCG Vaccine) in Healthy Infants

Conditions studied: BCG Vaccination Reaction, Tuberculosis
Lead sponsor: Green Cross Corporation
Overall status: COMPLETED
Study start date: 2019-08-06

STUDY DESIGN
Phase: Phase 3
Allocation: RANDOMIZED
Intervention model: PARALLEL
Primary purpose: PREVENTION
Masking: TRIPLE
Enrollment: 750 [Actual]

ELIGIBILITY
Ages eligible for study: — to 4 Weeks
Sexes eligible for study: ALL
Accepts healthy volunteers: Yes

PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURES
- Proportion of subjects with maximum diameter of induration ≥ 5mm in the tuberculin skin test [Time Frame: 84 days after administration of the investigational product]

BRIEF SUMMARY
The purpose of this study is to investigate the efficacy and safety of GC3107 in healthy infants
