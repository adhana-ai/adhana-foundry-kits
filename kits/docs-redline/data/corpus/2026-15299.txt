TITLE: Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and Plants; Endangered Species Status for Florida Keys Mole Skink; Correction
ACTION: Final rule; correction.

We, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Service), are correcting a final rule that appeared in the Federal Register on July 21, 2026. The rule added the Florida Keys mole skink (Plestiodon egregius egregius), a lizard subspecies from the Florida Keys, Florida, to the List of Endangered and Threatened Wildlife.
