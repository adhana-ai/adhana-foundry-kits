Reconcile three plan views and draft the gap brief

One planning cycle, one drafting call. Code aligns the three independently- maintained plan views for every line item and decides which gaps are material; the model tags a probable cause for each — or says unknown — cites two source lines, and drafts the narrative for the review meeting. Decision-free by design: nothing here ranks the plan views or recommends which one is right.

The cycles, their plan views and their planning notes are synthetic, generated at build time from a fixed seed — never written from memory.
