SUPPLIER AGREEMENT

Corvid Logistics Supply (SUP-0108)



The parties have agreed the following unit costs. For SKU-1800, the price is fixed at $22.82 per unit. For SKU-1801, the price is fixed at $29.06 per unit. For SKU-1802, the price is fixed at $24.89 per unit. For SKU-1803, the price is fixed at $34.89 per unit.

No line may be ordered below a floor of 240 units, and quantities are packed 12 to a carton -- round any partial carton up.

Payment terms are Net 30.

The supplier requires 10 business days between receiving a purchase order and releasing it for shipment. A shorter requested window cannot be met under the standing terms of this agreement.

Orders under this agreement's standing authorization must total between $500 and $50000; orders outside this band require a separate approval this agreement does not cover.
