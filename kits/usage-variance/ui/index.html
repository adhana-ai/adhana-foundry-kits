Reconcile a telecom invoice line against its mediated usage

One reconciliation record, one model call, a field table — plus a routing decision taken afterwards, in pure code, from two of the extracted values. The cause of a variance is arithmetic with a priority order: subtract the previous period’s late arrivals and the confirmed duplicates, keep the unrated usage, round up to the service’s own billing increment, and only then name what the remaining gap is. The analyst’s note is their remark on the line, and on this corpus it often names a cause the numbers do not support.

The flag is a business condition, not a check on the model: needs_credit fires when variance_cause is duplicate_records or late_records — the two that over-bill — and invoice_status is issued. Either variance on a draft can simply be corrected before it goes out; the same variance on an invoice already issued means the customer has paid for usage they do not owe. unrated_usage deliberately does not fire it: that is revenue leakage, and real, but nobody is owed money. This is the kit’s own simplification, not a real carrier’s billing-adjustment policy — no published tariff, settlement rule or credit procedure was consulted, and none is reproduced.
