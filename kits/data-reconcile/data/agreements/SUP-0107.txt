Millbrook Fabrication (SUP-0107)



The parties have agreed the following unit costs. For SKU-1700, the price is fixed at $23.58 per unit. For SKU-1701, the price is fixed at $29.18 per unit. For SKU-1702, the price is fixed at $28.07 per unit. For SKU-1703, the price is fixed at $30.68 per unit.

This agreement will not process a line quantity under 300 units. Carton size is 24 units; orders not landing on a carton multiple are rounded up by the supplier before shipment.

This agreement's distribution network covers the following regions only: APAC-EAST, EU-NORTH, NA-WEST. A destination outside that list is not served under these terms.
