Check a recurring entry and its reconciliation against the documented basis

One close cycle, one basis document, one model call, a verdict per check — with the clause it relied on. Three verdicts: clean, defect, and unverifiable. The third is the point: a basis document that never states a fact is not a pass, it is a gap, and calling it clean is how a silently-changed basis or an unexplained residual slips through unchecked.

This kit never posts, approves or clears anything — it only drafts verdicts for a preparer/reviewer to act on. The basis documents are synthetic, generated at build time from a fixed seed — never written from memory.
