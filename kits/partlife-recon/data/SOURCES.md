# Where this corpus came from

**It was written here.** `tools/build_corpus.py` generates every component record pack from a fixed
seed (`SEED = 20260822`), so the corpus is byte-identical on every rebuild and there is no
third-party data in this kit at all.

| | |
|---|---|
| source | generated by `tools/build_corpus.py`, seed `20260822` |
| dataset version | `partlife-recon-2026-08-22-50packs` |
| licence | **MIT** — this repository's own licence |
| record packs | 50, in `data/corpus/*.txt`, 41,929 bytes |
| gold | 50 rows in `data/gold.jsonl` |
| components / parts / airframes / locations | invented identifiers throughout, with no link to any real manufacturer, engine, part number, operator or aircraft |

## ⚠︎ What this kit is, and what it is not

**It reconciles records. It does not release anything to service.** The kit reconstructs the hours
and cycles a component's maintenance record trail substantiates, compares that against the pack's
published life limits, names every discrepancy and every gap, and escalates the packs that carry
one. **It never issues an airworthiness determination, a life-remaining certificate or a release to
service, and nothing in it is capable of doing so.** `life_status` is a statement about what the
RECORDS say; `escalate` raises a pack for a human, and a `no` on that flag means this rule found
nothing to raise — it does not mean the component may fly.

**No real airworthiness directive, maintenance manual, manufacturer limit or operator procedure is
named or reproduced anywhere in this kit.** "Life limit", "cycles since new", "time since
overhaul", "life-limited part", "return to service" and "records gap" are ordinary continuing-
airworthiness-records vocabulary; the *structure* of a record pack is modelled and nothing else.
Every life limit, rate, threshold and priority order below was invented for this corpus.

## Why an invented set of record packs, when real ones exist

**Because a real component record pack is not something this repo can publish either.** It names a
real operator, real airframes, a real part number and a specific person's review note, and it is
the primary evidence in a regulated airworthiness file. There is no public corpus of
(record trail, correctly-reconciled accumulated life) pairs, for the same reason there is no public
corpus of bank statements or proof-of-delivery records: the interesting cases are exactly the ones
nobody can publish.

Generating it also makes the label mechanical. Gold's `trail_hours` and `trail_cycles` are not
somebody's reading of a trail — they are the sum of the periods the document itself states, and
`evals/check_labels.py` re-reads them off the corpus text with a regex before any run is allowed to
spend.

## The mechanic: one call per pack, thirteen fields, two of them computed

Each pack states a component identifier, a part reference, the bonded store it is sitting in, the
published life limit in hours and cycles, the figures written on the component's own tag, a service
record trail, whether a records gap is declared, what disposition is being requested, and the
records reviewer's own note.

The model extracts thirteen fields — everything except the holding location, which no field maps to
and which is therefore never sent. **Eleven of the thirteen are copied. Two are computed**, and
those two are the kit: `trail_hours` and `trail_cycles` appear nowhere on the pack. They are the
sum of every installation period in the trail. See `src/prompt.py`.

## The rule, and the priority order a reader going quickly gets wrong

```
life_status(trail_hours, trail_cycles, limit_hours, limit_cycles, record_gap):
    at or past BOTH limits                    -> both_exceeded
    at or past the hours limit                -> hours_exceeded
    at or past the cycles limit               -> cycles_exceeded
    a records gap is declared                 -> cannot_determine   (checked LAST)
    otherwise                                  -> within_limits
```

| | |
|---|---|
| **the exceedance checks outrank the gap check** | a missing period of records can only **add** accumulated life — it can never bring a component the surviving records already put at or past a limit back inside it. So a gap makes *within limits* undeterminable and leaves *exceeded* perfectly determinable. **5 of the 12 gapped packs are already past a limit**, so this case is measured rather than assumed away |
| **the limit is inclusive** | exactly at the published limit there is no life remaining. **8 packs sit exactly ON a limit** |
| **the tag is a claim, not a measurement** | `life_status` is decided on the reconstructed trail totals and never on the figures written on the component's tag. **14 of 50 tags disagree** with what the records substantiate |

## The three arithmetic traps, all of them in the trail

1. **Hours and cycles are summed separately.** Every installation period in a pack ran on a
   different airframe at a **different hours-per-cycle ratio** — `evals/check_labels.py` asserts
   that no two periods within one pack share a ratio — so scaling one total off the other is wrong
   by construction.
2. **An overhaul resets one counter and not the other.** **16 packs** carry a line reading
   *"overhaul completed - time since overhaul reset to 0 hours / 0 cycles; time since new is
   unaffected and keeps accruing"*, with accruing periods on both sides of it (asserted, so
   restarting the count there always costs something). The life limit is against time since **new**.
3. **A declared gap contributes nothing.** A trail period marked `accrual NOT RECORDED` is stated
   and its accrual is not. The conservative answer is to say so, never to interpolate it from the
   periods either side or to fall back on the tag.

## The planted ambiguity: the reviewer's note is written in the wrong register

`N_NOTE_MISMATCH = 20` puts a records reviewer's note from the contradicting register on exactly
40% of packs:

- a component the trail does not clear, under *"Trail looks continuous on a quick read, filed
  without comment."*;
- a component comfortably inside both limits, under *"Not comfortable with this file yet, would
  want a supervisor over it first."*

Gold is **never** derived from the note. `evals/check_labels.py` asserts that **no note template
contains a digit or any of the words hour, cycle, limit, exceed, within, gap or tag** — so the note
cannot be evidence about anything even by accident, and any reader that leans on it is taking a
shortcut that this corpus is built to catch.

## The composition is exact, then shuffled — not drawn per record

Each class is a fixed **count**, shuffled by the seeded RNG, rather than an independent coin per
record. The corpus is still deterministic and byte-identical on every run; what changes is that the
numbers on the page are the numbers the design asked for. The mix on this seed:

| | |
|---|---|
| `within_limits` | 24 |
| `cycles_exceeded` | 7 |
| `hours_exceeded` | 6 |
| `both_exceeded` | 6 |
| `cannot_determine` | 7 |
| **not cleared by the trail (the positive class)** | **26** |
| packs declaring a records gap | 12 |
| — of those, already past a limit anyway (the sharpest case) | 5 |
| packs sitting exactly ON a published limit | 8 |
| tags disagreeing with the reconstructed total | 14 |
| packs carrying an overhaul line mid-trail | 16 |
| reviewer notes in the contradicting register | 20 (40%) |
| discrepancy **and** up for return to service — the escalate flag | 19 |

Installation periods per pack: 2 to 4, plus a declared gap period on 12 of them.

## The guardrail is a business condition, not a check on the model

`src/extract.py::compute()` escalates a pack when it carries a discrepancy — the trail does not put
the component inside both limits, **or** the component's own tag disagrees with the trail — **and**
the requested disposition is *return to service*. The same discrepancy on a component headed for
shelf storage still has to be resolved; it does not have to be resolved before an aircraft moves.

⚠︎ **This is the kit's own simplification, not any operator's or authority's release procedure.**
No airworthiness regulation, approved maintenance organisation exposition or continuing-
airworthiness management procedure was consulted, and none is reproduced. A real records desk
weighs which discrepancy it is, what evidence can still be recovered, and who is authorised to
accept what; this is three fields and a boolean, chosen because it is the smallest rule that is
genuinely useful and readable off one reply.

## What it cannot tell you

⚠︎ **This kit reconciles and escalates only — it never releases, certifies, quarantines or scraps
anything.** `life_status` is an extracted field and `escalate` is a routing signal; nothing here
disposes of anything, and a component this kit does not flag has not been cleared by it.

⚠︎ **An invented corpus contains the ambiguity we thought to plant and no others.** A real records
queue carries confusions this one does not attempt: a trail whose periods overlap or disagree with
each other, an accrual stated in one unit on one page and another elsewhere, a component whose life
limit was revised by a later directive, a repaired part whose life was formally re-established, a
sub-assembly whose own children each carry their own limits, or a scanned trail whose figures are
handwritten. A model that clears this corpus has cleared what this corpus tests, not every way an
accumulation can go wrong.

⚠︎ **And every figure in it is plain text in one consistent layout.** A real pack is a scan, a
photograph, or a printout from a system nobody here has seen.
