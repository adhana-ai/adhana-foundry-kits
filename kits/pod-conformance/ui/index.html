Check a proof-of-delivery record against what was actually booked

One delivery record, one model call, a field table — plus a routing decision taken afterwards, in pure code, from two of the extracted values. Completeness is a comparison: the units scanned at the drop against the units ordered, and the condition code the scan recorded. The driver’s exception note is their account of the stop, and on this corpus it often reads the opposite way from what actually happened.

The flag is a business condition, not a check on the model: needs_review fires when delivery_complete is no and recipient_signature_present is no. A shortfall somebody signed for is already acknowledged; the same shortfall with no signature has nothing on file to show the recipient was ever told. This is the kit’s own simplification, not a real carrier’s claims-intake policy — no published SLA or tariff was consulted, and none is reproduced.
