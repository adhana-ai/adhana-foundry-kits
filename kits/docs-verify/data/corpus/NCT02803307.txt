CLINICAL TRIAL RECORD
Registry identifier: NCT02803307

Title: Single-Dose Administration Trial of TLC599 in Osteoarthritis of the Knee

Conditions studied: Osteoarthritis of the Knee
Lead sponsor: Taiwan Liposome Company
Overall status: COMPLETED
Study start date: 2015-06

STUDY DESIGN
Phase: Phase 1/Phase 2
Allocation: RANDOMIZED
Intervention model: PARALLEL
Primary purpose: TREATMENT
Masking: NONE
Enrollment: 40 [Actual]

ELIGIBILITY
Ages eligible for study: 20 Years to N/A
Sexes eligible for study: ALL
Accepts healthy volunteers: No

PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURES
- Number of Participants With Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs) [Time Frame: up to 12 weeks after dosing]

BRIEF SUMMARY
This trial is a multi-site, randomized, open-label, parallel, and single-dose administration study to investigate the safety and efficacy of TLC599 in subjects with osteoarthritis of the knee.
