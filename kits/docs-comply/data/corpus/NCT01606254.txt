CLINICAL TRIAL REGISTRATION RECORD
Registry identifier: NCT01606254

IDENTIFICATION
Brief Title: A Pharmacokinetic and Safety Study of Paliperidone Palmitate (JNS010) in Participants With Schizophrenia
Official Title: A Repeated Dose Study of JNS010 (Paliperidone Palmitate) in Patients With Schizophrenia
Unique Protocol Identification Number: CR013612
Secondary ID: PALM-JPN-2

DESCRIPTION
Brief Summary: The purpose of this study is to assess the pharmacokinetics (the study of the way a drug enters and leaves the blood and tissues over time) and safety of paliperidone palmitate in participants with schizophrenia (psychiatric disorder with symptoms of emotional instability, detachment from reality, often with delusions and hallucinations, and withdrawal into the self).
Primary Disease or Condition Being Studied in the Trial: Schizophrenia

STUDY DESIGN
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Study Phase: PHASE2
Primary Purpose: TREATMENT
Study Design: RANDOMIZED; PARALLEL; NONE
Enrollment: 56 [Actual]

INTERVENTIONS
Intervention Name: Paliperidone palmitate
Intervention Type: DRUG
Intervention Description: Paliperidone palmitate aqueous suspension for injection 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 milliliter equivalent to 50, 100, 150 mg paliperidone respectively as intramuscular injection on Days 1, 8, 36 and 64.
Other Intervention Name: JNS010

OUTCOME MEASURES
Primary Outcome Measure Information: Paliperidone Pre-dose Plasma Concentration (Cpredose) at Day 8 [Time Frame: Day 8]
Primary Outcome Measure Information: Paliperidone Pre-dose Plasma Concentration (Cpredose) at Day 36 [Time Frame: Day 36]
Primary Outcome Measure Information: Paliperidone Pre-dose Plasma Concentration (Cpredose) at Day 64 [Time Frame: Day 64]
Primary Outcome Measure Information: Paliperidone Pre-dose Plasma Concentration (Cpredose) at Day 92 [Time Frame: Day 92]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale (PANSS) Total Score [Time Frame: Baseline, Day 8, 22, 50, 78 and 92]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Number of Participants With Clinical Global Impression Severity (CGI-S) Score [Time Frame: Baseline, Day 8, 22, 50, 78 and 92]
