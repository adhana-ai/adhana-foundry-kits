TITLE: Safety Zone; Ohio River; River, Aurora, IN
ACTION: Temporary final rule.

The Coast Guard is establishing a temporary safety zone for navigable waters on the Ohio River, from mile marker (MM) 499 to MM 497 in Aurora, Indiana. The safety zone is needed to protect personnel, vessels, and the marine environment from potential hazards associated with an over water powerline replacement. Entry of vessels or persons into this zone is prohibited unless specifically authorized by the Captain of the Port, Ohio Valley, or their designated representative.
