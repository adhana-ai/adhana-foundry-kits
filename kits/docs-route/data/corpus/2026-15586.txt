TITLE: Removal of References to the Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures in Federal Personnel Regulations
ACTION: Interim final rule; request for comments.

The Office of Personnel Management (OPM) is issuing an interim final rule with request for comments to remove references to the Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures (UGESP) from Federal civil service regulations. These amendments conform OPM's regulations to the Department of Justice, Office of Legal Counsel's June 9, 2026, opinion finding the UGESP unlawful.
