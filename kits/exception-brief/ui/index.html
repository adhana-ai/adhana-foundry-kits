Assemble the evidence packet and draft the exception brief

One review batch, one drafting call. Code flags which item/location exceptions are material -- recent POS versus the statistical forecast, or evidence itself flagged unreliable -- and packs the evidence (recent POS, lost-sales/OOS flags, the promo calendar, the prior-year analog) with the merchant notes log. The model tags a probable cause for each -- or says unknown -- cites two source lines, and drafts the narrative for the review meeting. Decision-free by design: nothing here recommends accepting or overriding the statistical forecast.

The batches, their evidence packets and their merchant notes are synthetic, generated at build time from a fixed seed — never written from memory.
