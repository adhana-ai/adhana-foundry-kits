Cross-check one seller application against its documents

One onboarding application, seven declared-vs-document field pairs — business identity, banking details, owner identity — one model call, a match / mismatch / mismatch_explained flag per field and a summary drafted for a verification analyst.

This kit never approves, denies, or escalates an application — it only extracts and cross-checks, and no flagged inconsistency is ever auto-cleared, even one that looks minor. Every application is synthetic, generated at build time from a fixed seed — never a real business or person.
