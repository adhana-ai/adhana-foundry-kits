Constrained supply allocation, drafted for review

One shortage event, one written policy (protect promo commitments, protect customer commitments, fair-share the rest by trailing velocity, check a 40% equity floor) — all run in code. The model never proposes a unit count. It reads the flagged events and the merchant notes log, tags a probable cause for each — or says unknown — cites two source lines, and drafts the narrative for the review meeting. Decision-free by design.

The sessions, their events and their merchant notes are synthetic, generated at build time from a fixed seed — never written from memory.
