Decide whether a warranty claim is actually covered, before it is paid

One dealer warranty claim, one model call, a field table — plus a recovery decision taken afterwards, in pure code. Coverage is six checks in a fixed order, and the last of them uses the plan’s own month and mileage limits, never the 3/36 everybody quotes. The technician’s narrative is the longest thing on the record and it ends with their own guess about whether the claim will pay; on this corpus that guess is wrong on 22 of 55 claims.

The flag is a business condition, not a check on the model: needs_review fires when covered is no and claim_status is paid. An uncovered claim still in submitted can simply be denied before any money moves; the same claim already paid means the money is out the door and somebody has to open a recovery against the dealer. This is the kit’s own simplification, not any manufacturer’s warranty-recovery policy — no published warranty programme, dealer agreement or chargeback procedure was consulted, and none is reproduced.
