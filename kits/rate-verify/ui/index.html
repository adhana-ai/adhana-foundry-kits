Check whether a utility bill's applied rate matches what the account qualifies for

One billing account record, one model call, a field table — plus a routing decision taken afterwards, in pure code, from two of the extracted values. Rate correctness is a four-value comparison with a priority order: service class first, then whether an interval meter and high usage earn a time-of-use rate regardless of demand, then the demand threshold. The billing rep’s own account note is their remark on the account, and on this corpus it often reads the opposite way from what the values say.

The flag is a business condition, not a check on the model: needs_review fires when rate_correct is no and bill_status is sent. A misrated account still in draft can simply be corrected before it goes out; the same mismatch on a bill already sent means the customer needs a corrected bill or a credit. This is the kit’s own simplification, not a real utility’s billing-adjustment policy — no published tariff or regulatory rule was consulted, and none is reproduced.
