TITLE: Proposed Information Collection Activity; Formative Data Collections for ACF Research and Evaluation
ACTION: Request for public comments.

The Administration for Children and Families (ACF) proposes to extend data collection under the existing overarching generic clearance for Formative Data Collections for ACF Research and Evaluation (Office of Management and Budget (OMB)#: 0970-0356). There are no changes proposed to the intended purpose or use of the potential data collections, but burden has been adjusted to reflect agency priorities.
