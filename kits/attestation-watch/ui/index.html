Whose attestation is missing, stale, or contradicted by what else is on file

One engagement’s independence attestation register, one model call, a roster back — plus a status per person taken afterwards, in pure code, from the extracted values. Five gates in order: is this person required to attest at all, can the register even be read on them, is there a return, does the register contradict itself, and only then was the return in time and about the right window. The due date is derived, never read — no register states one. The administrator’s note is on the page and decides nothing.

This kit watches nothing. It reads one snapshot somebody else assembled and proposes a worklist. It does not poll, subscribe, schedule, alert, escalate, chase, file, sign a register off or clear anybody, and nothing in it runs unattended. It proposes a status with its reasoning, names what it could not determine, and never authorises, writes, dispatches, releases or clears anything. And the rulebook it decides by is invented. It was written for this kit and reproduces no real professional standard, standard-setter’s rule, regulator’s requirement or firm policy. Replace it with your own before you route anything real by it.

The routing flag is a business condition, not a check on the model: needs_owner_review fires when the register carries at least one contradicted person or at least one not_determinable one. A missing or stale attestation is cleared by asking somebody for a form; a contradiction and an unreadable record are not, and routing them to the same queue as a chase is how they get buried under it. This is the kit’s own simplification, not anybody’s escalation policy — no firm’s procedure, professional standard or regulatory rule was consulted, and none is reproduced.
