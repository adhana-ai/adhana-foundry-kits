CLINICAL TRIAL RECORD
Registry identifier: NCT01359696

Title: A Study Evaluating the Safety, Tolerability, and Pharmacokinetics of GDC-0425 Administered With and Without Gemcitabine in Patients With Refractory Solid Tumors or Lymphoma

Conditions studied: Solid Tumor
Lead sponsor: Genentech, Inc.
Overall status: COMPLETED
Study start date: 2011-07-11

STUDY DESIGN
Phase: Phase 1
Allocation: NA
Intervention model: SINGLE_GROUP
Primary purpose: TREATMENT
Masking: NONE
Enrollment: 40 [Actual]

ELIGIBILITY
Ages eligible for study: 18 Years to N/A
Sexes eligible for study: ALL
Accepts healthy volunteers: No

PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURES
- Incidence of all adverse events graded according to NCI CTCAE, v4.0 [Time Frame: Up to 1 year]
- Incidence of dose limiting toxicities (DLTs) [Time Frame: Up to 35 days]
- Pharmacokinetic property of GDC-0425: determination of plasma drug concentration [Time Frame: Up to 12 months or early study discontinuation]

BRIEF SUMMARY
This is an open-label, multicenter, Phase I, dose-escalation study to assess the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics (PK), and pharmacodynamics (PD) of GDC-0425 administered with and without gemcitabine.
