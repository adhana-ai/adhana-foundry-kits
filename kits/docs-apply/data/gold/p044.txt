IVORYDALE FOODS — TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Document ref: POL-6541   ·   Version 4
Effective: 2026-02-15

1. Scope
   This policy applies to all employees and contractors of Ivorydale Foods.

2. Approval
   Any commitment above 500 EUR requires written approval from a line manager before it is made.

3. Notice
   Requests must be raised at least 10 working days before the date they take effect.

4. Submission
   Completed records must be submitted within 14 days of the event they describe.

5. Retention
   Records under this document are retained for 36 months and then destroyed.

6. Exceptions
   Any exception to clause 4 must be approved in writing by the Compliance team and recorded on the exception register.

Issued by the Operations Office. Questions to operations@ivorydale.example
