CLINICAL TRIAL RECORD
Registry identifier: NCT03702738

Title: Adjunctive NAC in Adult Patients With Pulmonary Tuberculosis

Conditions studied: Tuberculosis, Pulmonary
Lead sponsor: The Aurum Institute NPC
Overall status: COMPLETED
Study start date: 2019-03-01

STUDY DESIGN
Phase: Phase 2
Allocation: RANDOMIZED
Intervention model: PARALLEL
Primary purpose: TREATMENT
Masking: NONE
Enrollment: 110 [Actual]

ELIGIBILITY
Ages eligible for study: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sexes eligible for study: ALL
Accepts healthy volunteers: No

PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURES
- Median time to stable sputum culture conversion using liquid medium [Time Frame: 6 months]

BRIEF SUMMARY
To determine if adjunctive N-acetylcysteine 1200 mg twice a day (BID) accelerates sputum culture conversion and normalization of cellular glutathione in tuberculosis (TB), and to assess its potential effects on lung and immune function
