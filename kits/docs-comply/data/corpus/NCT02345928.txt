CLINICAL TRIAL REGISTRATION RECORD
Registry identifier: NCT02345928

IDENTIFICATION
Brief Title: A Single Ascending Dose Study in Healthy Participants and Multiple Ascending Dose Study of CNTO 7160 in Participants With Asthma and Participants With Atopic Dermatitis
Official Title: A Phase 1, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Single Ascending Dose Study in Healthy Subjects and Multiple Ascending Dose Study of CNTO 7160 in Subjects With Asthma and Subjects With Atopic Dermatitis
Unique Protocol Identification Number: CR105130
Secondary ID: CNTO7160ASH1001; 2014-000633-23

DESCRIPTION
Brief Summary: The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety and tolerability of single ascending dose of CNTO 7160 administered intravenously (IV) in healthy participants and multiple dose administered IV in participants with asthma and atopic dermatitis.
Primary Disease or Condition Being Studied in the Trial: Asthma, Atopic Dermatitis, Healthy

STUDY DESIGN
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Study Phase: PHASE1
Primary Purpose: TREATMENT
Study Design: RANDOMIZED; SINGLE_GROUP; DOUBLE
Enrollment: 108 [Actual]

INTERVENTIONS
Intervention Name: Part 1: CNTO 7160
Intervention Type: DRUG
Intervention Description: Participants will receive Drug CNTO7160 administered IV infusion at escalating doses (with a starting dose of 0.001 mg/kg).
Intervention Name: Part 2 (Asthma): CNTO 7160
Intervention Type: DRUG
Intervention Description: Participants will receive Drug CNTO 7160 administered IV infusions (3 dose administrations over 4 weeks).
Intervention Name: Part 2 (Atopic Dermatitis): CNTO 7160
Intervention Type: DRUG
Intervention Description: Participants will receive Drug CNTO 7160 administered IV infusions (3 dose administrations over 4 weeks).
Intervention Name: Part 1 and Part 2: Placebo
Intervention Type: DRUG
Intervention Description: Participants will receive single IV infusion of placebo matched to CNTO 7160.

OUTCOME MEASURES
Primary Outcome Measure Information: Number of Participants with Treatment Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs) as a Measure of Safety and Tolerability of CNTO 7160 (Part 1) [Time Frame: Through Week 17]
Primary Outcome Measure Information: Number of Participants with Treatment Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs) as a Measure of Safety and Tolerability of CNTO 7160 (Part 2) [Time Frame: Through Week 21]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Maximum Observed Serum Concentration (Cmax) (Part 1) [Time Frame: Up to Week 17 after dose]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Maximum Observed Serum Concentration (Cmax) (Part 2) [Time Frame: Up to Week 21 after dose]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Area Under the Serum Time Curve [AUC(0-t) and AUC(0-infinity)] (Part 1) [Time Frame: Up to Week 17 after dose]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Area Under the Serum Concentration Versus Time Curve [AUC(t1-t2)] (Part 2) [Time Frame: Up to Week 21 after dose]
