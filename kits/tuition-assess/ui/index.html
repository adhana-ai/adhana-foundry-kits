Check whether a student account’s assessed tuition and fees match the rate table

One student-account tuition assessment, one model call, a field table — plus a routing decision taken afterwards, in pure code, from two of the extracted values. Correctness here is arithmetic with an order: the credit band first (twelve credits is full-time, and the flat band is cheaper than twelve at the per-credit rate), then the course-level differential fee, then the mandatory fee, then the waiver, which is applied last and does not cover everything. Two lines on the record are there to be copied and not acted on — a mid-term residency reclassification that takes effect next term, and the bursar’s own note, which on this corpus often reads the opposite way from what the arithmetic says.

The flag is a business condition, not a check on the model: needs_review fires when assessment_correct is no and bill_status is posted. A mis-assessed account still in draft can simply be corrected before it posts; the same variance on a posted bill means a corrected bill or a refund against a balance the student may already have paid. “Rate table says” is not a grade — it is the same arithmetic re-run over the model’s own extracted values, so it needs no labels and it is blind to a reply that misreads a value and then judges that misreading correctly. This rate table is the kit’s own invention, not any institution’s published schedule — no real tuition schedule, fee bill, waiver programme or residency regulation was consulted, and none is reproduced. Every record here is synthetic; no real student data of any kind is in this repository.
