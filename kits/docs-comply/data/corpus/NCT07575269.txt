IDENTIFICATION
Brief Title: Evaluation of the FARAFLEX Mapping and Pulsed Field Ablation System in Subjects With Atrial Fibrillation
Official Title: Evaluation of the FARAFLEX Mapping and Pulsed Field Ablation System in Subjects With Atrial Fibrillation - A Global Prospective Single Arm Study
Unique Protocol Identification Number: PF314

DESCRIPTION
Brief Summary: The goal of this clinical trial is to learn if the FARAFLEX™ Mapping and pulsed field ablation (PFA) System can safely and effectively treat symptomatic, drug-refractory Paroxysmal atrial fibrillation (PAF) and Persistent atrial fibrillation (PersAF). The main question it aims to answer is: Is the FARAFLEX Mapping and PFA System a safe and effective treatment for patients? Participants will undergo an ablation procedure using the FARAFLEX Mapping and PFA System.
Primary Disease or Condition Being Studied in the Trial: Paroxysmal Atrial Fibrillation (PAF), Persistent Atrial Fibrillation

OUTCOME MEASURES
Primary Outcome Measure Information: Proportion of Treatment Subjects and Attempt Subjects with device- or procedure-related defined Composite Serious Adverse Events, assessed through Day 60 following the index procedure. [Time Frame: After the index procedure, rescheduled index procedure, or first re-ablation performed with the FARAFLEX (Day 0) through Day 60]
Primary Outcome Measure Information: The primary effectiveness endpoint (PEE) is the proportion of Treatment Subjects with Treatment Success through the Day 365 Assessment. [Time Frame: Day 0 through the Day 365 Assessment]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Atrial Arrhythmia Burden Success Endpoint [Time Frame: Post-blanking period (Day 60) through 6 months (Day 180)]
Secondary Outcome Measure Information: Cavo-Tricuspid Isthmus (CTI) Ablation - Acute Procedural Endpoint [Time Frame: Index Ablation Procedure]
