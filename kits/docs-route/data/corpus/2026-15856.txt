TITLE: Notice of Request for Approval of a New Information Collection; National Animal Health Monitoring System; Poultry 2027 Upland Gamebird and Duck Study
ACTION: Request for approval of a new information collection; comment request.

In accordance with the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, this notice announces the Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service's intention to request approval of a new information collection associated with the conduct of the National Animal Health Monitoring System's Upland Gamebird and Duck study in 2027.
