Assemble the lien-waiver coverage picture behind a progress payment

One payment package, one model call, a field table — plus a routing decision taken afterwards, in pure code, from two of the extracted values. Coverage is decided party by party by a five-condition rule with a priority order: no waiver at all, then a preliminary notice dated after the waiver was signed, then a progress waiver whose through-date stops short of the period, then an amount that does not reach, then a conditional waiver something has already cleared against. The payment coordinator’s own note is their remark on the package, and on this corpus it often reads the opposite way from what the party blocks say.

This assembles evidence and names the gaps. A person releases the payment. Nothing here determines anybody’s lien rights, and the coverage rule it applies is this kit’s own invention — no jurisdiction’s statute or statutory waiver form is reproduced.

A package with a gap that is already on hold is not news; the same gap on one scheduled to go out this cycle is the package a coordinator has to pull back today. It routes, it does not authorise — and a package with no gap is not thereby cleared for release, it is a package this kit found nothing to say about.
