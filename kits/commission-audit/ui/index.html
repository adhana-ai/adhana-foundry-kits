Check whether a channel’s commission claim is actually owed against the folio

One commission claim line joined to the property’s own folio record, one model call, a field table — plus a routing decision taken afterwards, in pure code, from two of the extracted values. Whether a claim is owed is a five-branch computation with a priority order: the booking source first, then whether the stay was already commissioned, then the commissionable base (room revenue net of refunds, or the cancellation penalty actually charged — never the taxes and fees beside them), then the contracted percentage. The property reviewer’s own note is their remark on the line, and on this corpus it often reads the opposite way from what the folio says.

The flag is a business condition, not a check on the model: needs_recovery fires when claim_valid is no and invoice_status is paid. A bad line on an unpaid invoice can simply be short-paid before settlement; the same line on an invoice already paid means the property is out of pocket and has to raise a recovery claim against the channel. This is the kit’s own simplification, not a real property’s dispute policy — no channel’s published dispute procedure or distribution agreement was consulted, and none is reproduced.
