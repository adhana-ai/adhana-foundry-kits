Draft a cycle-count variance's likely cause

One variance event, one drafting call. The item/location's own transaction history log — receiving, transfers, scans, adjustments — is all the model sees. It tags a probable cause from a fixed vocabulary — or says unresolved — cites the exact log line, and drafts a short note for inventory control. Decision-free by design: nothing here posts an adjustment or closes the variance.

The events, their transaction logs and their gold causes are synthetic, generated at build time from a fixed seed — never written from memory.
