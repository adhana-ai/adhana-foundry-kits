SUPPLIER AGREEMENT

Thornfield Parts Co (SUP-0109)



The parties have agreed the following unit costs. For SKU-1900, the price is fixed at $43.87 per unit. For SKU-1901, the price is fixed at $4.85 per unit. For SKU-1902, the price is fixed at $7.32 per unit.

The supplier requires 9 business days between receiving a purchase order and releasing it for shipment. A shorter requested window cannot be met under the standing terms of this agreement.

This agreement's distribution network covers the following regions only: NA-CENTRAL, NA-EAST. A destination outside that list is not served under these terms.

Orders under this agreement's standing authorization must total between $500 and $25000; orders outside this band require a separate approval this agreement does not cover.

This agreement will not process a line quantity under 240 units. Carton size is 25 units; orders not landing on a carton multiple are rounded up by the supplier before shipment.

Payment terms are Net 30.
