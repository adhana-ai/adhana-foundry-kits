SUPPLIER AGREEMENT

Underwood Metalworks (SUP-0110)



The parties have agreed the following unit costs. For SKU-2000, the price is fixed at $37.67 per unit. For SKU-2001, the price is fixed at $30.82 per unit. For SKU-2002, the price is fixed at $36.10 per unit.

No line may be ordered below a floor of 300 units, and quantities are packed 25 to a carton -- round any partial carton up.

Payment terms are Net 60.

This agreement's distribution network covers the following regions only: APAC-EAST, EU-SOUTH, NA-CENTRAL. A destination outside that list is not served under these terms.

Quoted lead time is 9 business days from purchase-order receipt to ship date; requested ship windows shorter than this are not feasible without an expedite agreement, which this contract does not grant.
