# Where each document came from

**Nowhere. We wrote all of them.**

Every file in `clean/` and `messy/` is generated by `src/corpus.py` from the fixed seed `20260814`.
No document here is copied, scraped, adapted or derived from any third-party corpus, and there is
no upstream licence to honour and no attribution owed. The whole corpus is MIT, granted by us.

## Why self-authored

Two reasons, and the second is the one that decided it:

1. **Real scanned enterprise documents cannot be published.** They are third-party, usually
   confidential, and their licences do not permit redistribution.
2. **This kit needs the same page in two conditions with one ground truth attached to both**, and
   no public corpus ships that pair. We know what each page said before it was damaged, because we
   wrote it before we damaged it.

## Verify it yourself

```bash
python3 tools/build_corpus.py --verify
```

That rebuilds all 120 files from the seed and diffs them byte for byte against what is committed
here. If it exits 0, the corpus on disk is exactly what the generator produces — which is the whole
of the provenance claim, and it is checkable rather than asserted.

## ⚠︎ What the `messy/` half is NOT

It is a **model** of scanner and OCR damage, not the output of a real scanner. Every error class in
`src/corpus.py` is one OCR demonstrably makes — confusable glyphs, swallowed spaces, dropped
punctuation, ligature collapse, page furniture landing mid-body, failed de-hyphenation — and the
rates land the corpus at a 2.6% character error rate. But no scanner and no OCR engine was
involved. No number this kit publishes should be read as measured against real scans.
